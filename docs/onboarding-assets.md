# Onboarding asset provenance

`app/public/assets/speakmode-mist.png` is the original atmospheric background
generated with the built-in image generation tool for the approved SpeakMode
motion prototype on 2026-10-17. It is 1024 × 1536 pixels; SHA256:
`b78ae2caa1a857fd85be277e696e5e0d6be59757f6aabd456bb2dd588f0ae2d9`.

The prompt requested a nearly white mist-blue abstract texture with translucent
wisps, quiet central space and low contrast, with no text, logos, UI, people or
recognizable objects. The supplied screenshots informed the mood. No competitor
screenshots, application logos or avatars are bundled in the application.

The existing SpeakMode Sans font and its OFL license remain in `app/public/fonts`.
Icons come from the existing `@radix-ui/react-icons` dependency. No new runtime
dependencies were added for onboarding or motion.
